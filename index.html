About Elyu Gateway

Your Gateway to Staying in La Union

Elyu Gateway is a simple directory designed to help travelers discover places to stay throughout La Union.

From affordable hostels and backpacker accommodations to transient houses and private rooms, the goal is to make finding your next stay easier.

Whether you're visiting for surfing, food, beaches, nightlife, work or simply a weekend escape, Elyu Gateway helps you find a place to call home while you're in Elyu.
